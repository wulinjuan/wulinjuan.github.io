Linjuan Wu

Researcher · Xiaohongshu

Exploring language.
Connecting knowledge.

I am a Researcher at Xiaohongshu, currently focusing on social media translation: helping language models convey meaning, cultural context, and emotion across languages. My broader interests include multilingual language models, cross-lingual transfer, and reasoning.

I received my Ph.D. from Zhejiang University in June 2026. I also contributed to the Qwen3 Technical Report.
